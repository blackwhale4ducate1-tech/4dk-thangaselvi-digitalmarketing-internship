## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
Ad Extensions (now called Assets in Google Ads) increase your ad's visibility, provide more information to search users, and improve click-through rates (CTR) by occupying more vertical space on search engine results pages.

This document contains copy for **6 Sitelinks**, **8 Callouts**, **Structured Snippets**, a **Call Extension**, and a **Lead Form Extension**. This copy is tailored for our Online Fitness Coaching business to address user objections and highlight our USPs.

## 🛠️ 3. Step-by-Step Configuration Instructions

1.  Open the Google Ads interface. Navigate to **Campaigns > Assets** (or **Ads & Assets > Assets**).
2.  Click the blue **+** button to create a new asset.
3.  Select **Sitelink**. Copy and paste Sitelinks 1-6 with their descriptions and final URLs. Associate them with your search campaigns.
4.  Select **Callout**. Enter Callouts 1-8. Save and apply.
5.  Select **Structured Snippet**. Create one for "Services" and one for "Types".
6.  Select **Lead Form**. Fill out the business name, headline, fields, and privacy policy. Map the submission data to your CRM via Zapier webhook.

## 🚫 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Duplicate Sitelink URLs:** Bending Google policies by pointing multiple sitelinks to the same landing page. Sitelink URLs must point to unique pages on your domain.
*   **Repeating Ad Copy in Callouts:** Copying text from your search ad descriptions into callouts. Keep callouts unique to avoid repetitive ads.
*   **Skipping Description Lines in Sitelinks:** Entering only the sitelink headline without descriptions. Description lines occupy more space on results pages and increase click-through rates.
