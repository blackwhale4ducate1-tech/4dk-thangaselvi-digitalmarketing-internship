# Campaign 02: Competitor Conquest Specification

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
Bidding on competitor brand names (like "CoPilot" or "Future Fitness") is a standard acquisition strategy in B2B and premium B2C SaaS. Users searching for competitor brands are already in the buying mindset; bidding on their brand names allows us to present our premium alternative and capture high-intent leads.

This campaign targets competitor search terms. It directs traffic to a dedicated **competitor comparison landing page** to highlight our USPs (weekly video check-ins and custom meal plans) directly against their offers.

---

## 📋 2. Campaign Configurations & Keywords

### A. Core Settings
*   **Daily Budget:** **$40.00/day** ($1,200/month).
*   **Bidding Strategy:** **Manual CPC** (at launch)
    *   *Rationale:* Google's smart bidding algorithms require historical conversion data to optimize. Manual CPC allows us to bid high enough ($3.50-$4.50) to win auctions against competitor brands early on.
    *   *Bid Limit:* Set maximum CPC bids to **$5.00** to avoid overspending on competitive terms.
*   **Networks:** Google Search Network only (Search Partners and Display Network disabled).

### B. Target Keywords
Add these keywords as **Exact Match** and **Phrase Match**:

1.  `"Future Fitness"` / `[Future Fitness]`
2.  `"CoPilot Fitness"` / `[CoPilot Fitness]`
3.  `"Caliber Coaching"` / `[Caliber Coaching]`
4.  `"Kickoff Online Coaching"` / `[Kickoff Online Coaching]`
5.  `"Precision Nutrition Coaching"` / `[Precision Nutrition Coaching]`

## 🛠️ 3. Step-by-Step Launch & Policy Compliance Instructions

1.  **Create Campaign:** Add a new search campaign named `02_Search_Competitor_US-UK-CA`.
2.  **Organize Ad Groups:** Create separate ad groups for each competitor (e.g., `Competitor_Future`, `Competitor_CoPilot`) to write highly targeted copy.
3.  **Configure Bidding:** Set the bidding strategy to Manual CPC with "Help increase conversions with Enhanced CPC" enabled. Set starting bids to $4.00.
4.  **Policy Compliance (Crucial):** **Do not use competitor brand names in your ad headlines or body copy.** Bidding on competitor terms is allowed, but using their trademarked names in your ad copy violates Google Ads policy and will result in your ads being taken down. Focus copy on your own USPs (e.g., "Tired of App Message Bots? Get a Real 1-on-1 Fitness Coach").
5.  **Configure Extensions:** Enable sitelinks pointing to our features comparison page.
6.  **Create Ads:** Upload the Responsive Search Ads from [AD_GROUP_02.md](file:///d:/4DK/Projects/Marketting/google-ads-ppc-fitness/ad-copy/AD_GROUP_02.md).

## 🚫 5. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Violating Trademark Policies:** Using competitor brand names in your ad headlines. Keep ad copy focused on your own USPs.
*   **Directing Traffic to Your Homepage:** Sending competitor search traffic to your homepage instead of a dedicated comparison landing page. Visitors looking for a competitor need to see a direct comparison to understand why your service is a better alternative.
*   **Underbidding:** Setting bids too low. Competitors bid high on their own brand terms; if your bids are too low, your ads will not show. Set starting bids to $3.50-$4.50.
