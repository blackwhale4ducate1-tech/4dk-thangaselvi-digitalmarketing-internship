# Campaign 03: Service & Intent Search Specification

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
This campaign is the primary driver of new coaching leads. It targets users actively searching for professional online fitness and nutrition coaching services (e.g., "online fitness coach", "hire personal trainer online").

These users have high commercial intent but are not yet committed to a specific brand. We organize these keywords into distinct, themed ad groups to ensure we serve highly relevant ad copy and direct traffic to tailored landing pages.

---

## 📋 2. Campaign Configurations & Keywords

### A. Core Settings
*   **Daily Budget:** **$110.00/day** ($3,300/month).
*   **Bidding Strategy:** **Manual CPC with Enhanced CPC (eCPC)** enabled at launch.
    *   *Rationale:* Focuses on winning core service auctions at a reasonable cost-per-click ($2.50 - $3.50) while building historical conversion data.
    *   *Transition Plan:* Once the campaign registers **30 conversions in 30 days**, we will transition to **Maximize Conversions** with a Target CPA of **$50.00**.
*   **Networks:** Google Search Network only (Search Partners and Display Network disabled).

## 🚫 5. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Bidding on Broad Informational Queries:** Adding keywords like "workout tips" or "how to lose weight". These queries have informational intent and will waste your budget on visitors who do not want to hire a coach.
*   **Ignoring Ad Group Relevance:** Sending users searching for "diet coaching" to a landing page that focuses exclusively on weightlifting workouts. Match your ad groups, ad copy, and landing pages to the user's specific search query.
*   **Neglecting Search Terms Reports:** Failing to review search terms reports weekly. Reviewing search terms reports is essential for identifying and adding new negative keywords to exclude unwanted traffic.
