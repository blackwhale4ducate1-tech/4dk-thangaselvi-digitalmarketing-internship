# Campaign 01: Brand Protection Specification

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
If you do not bid on your own brand name, competitors will. Competitors frequently bid on terms like "FitLife Coaching" to capture high-intent leads who are looking for us. Since brand search clicks are cheap and convert at a high rate, bidding on our own brand protects our top position on search engine results pages (SERPs).

This campaign targets our brand keywords. It uses a **Target Impression Share** bidding strategy to ensure we maintain our top ranking for all brand searches.

---

## 📋 2. Campaign Configurations & Keywords

### A. Core Settings
*   **Daily Budget:** **$20.00/day** ($600/month).
*   **Bidding Strategy:** **Target Impression Share**
    *   *Percent Impression Share Target:* **95%**
    *   *Where to Show:* **Anywhere on the results page** (or **Top of results page**).
    *   *Maximum CPC Bid Limit:* **$2.00** (caps bidding costs during competitor bidding wars).
*   **Networks:** Google Search Network only (Search Partners disabled, Display Network disabled).

### B. Target Keywords
Add these keywords as **Exact Match** and **Phrase Match**:

1.  `[FitLife Coaching]`
2.  `"FitLife Coaching"`
3.  `[FitLife Online Fitness]`
4.  `"FitLife Online Fitness"`
5.  `[FitLife Personal Trainer]`
6.  `"FitLife Personal Trainer"`

---

## 🛠️ 3. Step-by-Step Launch Instructions

1.  **Create Campaign:** Add a new search campaign named `01_Search_Brand_US-UK-CA`.
2.  **Apply Keywords:** Add the brand keywords listed above to a single ad group named `Brand_Core`.
3.  **Configure Bidding:** Set the bidding strategy to Target Impression Share with a 95% target top-of-page share and a $2.00 max CPC limit.
4.  **Add Negative List:** Apply your global negative list to exclude irrelevant searches.
5.  **Configure Extensions:** Enable sitelinks and callouts pointing to our Pricing and About pages.
6.  **Create Ads:** Upload the Responsive Search Ads from [AD_GROUP_01.md](file:///d:/4DK/Projects/Marketting/google-ads-ppc-fitness/ad-copy/AD_GROUP_01.md).

## 🚫 5. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Bidding on Brand in Broad Match:** Adding your brand name as a broad match keyword (e.g., `FitLife`). This triggers ads for unrelated terms containing "fit" or "life", wasting your brand budget.
*   **Not Setting a Max CPC Limit:** Forgetting to set a Maximum CPC limit. During competitor bidding wars, CPCs for your own brand can spike to $5+, wasting budget. A $2.00 limit keeps click costs low.
*   **Sending Brand Traffic to Lead Forms Only:** Directing brand searches to a bare-bones lead capture landing page. Users searching for your brand name are looking for your main website; send them to a page where they can access your main navigation (like your Homepage or dedicated Brand Landing Page).
