# PPC Weekly Campaign Performance Report

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
This document serves as a template for compiling and sharing weekly Google Ads performance updates with stakeholders or clients. 

When preparing reports, delete these instruction sections and fill in the placeholders (indicated by brackets `[...]`) with real data. The report tracks budget pacing, key PPC metrics, and documents the optimization actions taken during the week to maintain account efficiency.

---

## 🛠️ 2. Step-by-Step Instructions: How to Complete This Report

1.  **Extract Weekly Metrics:** Set the date range in Google Ads to the preceding week (e.g. Monday to Sunday). Collect spend, clicks, impressions, conversion counts, and average CPAs.
2.  **Calculate Budget Pacing:** Check if your spend matches your monthly budget allocation ($200/day average). Highlight any budget over- or under-spends.
3.  **Log Optimizations:** Document account updates made during the week (e.g. negative keywords added, bidding changes, ad copy testing).
4.  **Complete the Placeholders:** Replace the bracketed text with your metrics and data.
5.  **Distribute the Report:** Export this document to PDF or share it in your team channel every Monday.

## 🚫 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Hiding Cost-Per-Lead Spikes:** Omitting temporary CPA increases in reports. Always present performance data transparently and document the actions you are taking to resolve issues.
*   **No Action Items for Next Week:** Reporting performance without listing optimization steps. Always explain *what* you will do next to improve performance.
*   **Confusing Weekly Spikes with Monthly Trends:** Making major campaign changes based on a single poor performance day. Look at rolling 7-day or 30-day averages before adjusting bids.
