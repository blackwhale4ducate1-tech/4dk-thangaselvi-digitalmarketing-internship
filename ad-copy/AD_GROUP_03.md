# Ad Group 03: Service & Intent Ad Copy (Ready to Paste)

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
This document contains the ad copy for **Ad Group 03: Service & Intent**. These ads target users searching for non-brand terms like "hire online fitness coach" and "online personal trainer". Since these visitors do not know our brand yet, our copy focuses on aligning with their search queries, highlighting our premium USPs, and establishing credibility.

Each Responsive Search Ad (RSA) is optimized for specific search themes: general personal training, 1-on-1 accountability for busy careers, and integrated nutrition/diet coaching.

## 🛠️ 3. Step-by-Step Upload Instructions

2.  Select the **Ads** tab and click the blue **+** button to create a new **Responsive Search Ad**.

4.  **Implement Pinning:** Pin Headline 1 to Position 1, Headline 2 to Position 2, and Headline 9 to Position 3.
5.  Save and repeat for RSA 2 and RSA 3.

## 🚫 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Headline Truncation:** Writing headlines over 30 characters. Double-check character counts before saving.
*   **Irrelevant Landing Pages:** Sending users who search for "nutrition coaching" to a workout-only landing page. Match your landing pages to the specific ad group themes.
*   **Over-Pinning Headlines:** Pinning all headlines. This prevents Google's algorithm from optimizing headline combinations. Only pin the brand names, core services, and CTAs.
