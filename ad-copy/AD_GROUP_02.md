# Ad Group 02: Competitor Conquest Ad Copy (Ready to Paste)

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
These ads target users searching for competitor platforms (Future, CoPilot, Caliber). Since these users are looking for alternative solutions, our ad copy focuses on addressing competitor weaknesses—such as automated messages, lack of nutrition customization, or requirement of proprietary hardware (wearables).

**IMPORTANT POLICY COMPLIANCE:** To comply with Google Ads policies and avoid trademark disputes, **do not mention competitor brand names anywhere in your ad headlines or body copy.** Bidding on competitor keywords is allowed, but using their trademarked names in your copy is a policy violation.

## 🚫 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Including Competitor Trademarks in Headlines:** Using trademarked competitor names. While you can target their keywords, including their name in your ad copy can lead to trademark flags and ad disapprovals.
*   **Bidding on Competitors with a Value Prop Mismatch:** Targeting low-cost competitors without explaining why your premium service justifies its price. Highlight features like "weekly video calls" to explain the premium value.
*   **Directing Traffic to a Generic Homepage:** Sending users to your main homepage. Ensure they arrive on a dedicated competitor comparison landing page.
