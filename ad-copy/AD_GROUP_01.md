This document contains the ad copy for **Ad Group 01: Brand Core**. These ads target users searching directly for our brand name ("FitLife"). Since these users already know us, the copy focuses on brand validation, our core USPs, and a direct call to action to book a strategy call.

Each Responsive Search Ad (RSA) contains **10 Headlines** and **4 Descriptions** that Google's algorithm will dynamically test. We use a **pinning strategy** to ensure our brand name always appears in the first headline position.

## 🛠3. Step-by-Step Upload Instructions

1.  Open the Google Ads interface and navigate to your **Brand Protection** campaign.
2.  Select the **Ads** tab and click the blue **+** button to create a new **Responsive Search Ad**.
3.  Input the headlines and descriptions for RSA 1 exactly as written.
4.  **Implement Pinning:** Click the pin icon next to Headline 1 and select "Show only in position 1". Pin Headline 2 to position 2, and Headline 9 to position 3.
5.  Save and repeat for RSA 2 and RSA 3.

---

## 🚫 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Headline Truncation:** Writing headlines over 30 characters. Google Ads will block these from saving.
*   **Over-Pinning Headlines:** Pinning all 10 headlines to position 1. This prevents Google from testing combinations and lowers your Ad Strength rating. Pin only the critical brand and CTA headlines.
*   **Missing Trademark Symbols:** Forgetting to add `™` or `®` to your brand headlines, which helps establish authority.
