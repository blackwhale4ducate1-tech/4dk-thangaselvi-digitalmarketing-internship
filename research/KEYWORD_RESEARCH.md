# Keyword Research Methodology & Strategy

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
For a high-ticket online fitness coaching business ($150-$300/month), search intent is the difference between a high-converting campaign and a wasted ad budget. Users searching for "free workout plans" or "fitness tips" are looking for quick information, not high-ticket 1-on-1 coaching. 

This document explains our keyword research methodology, tool configuration, and search intent grouping. It provides a standardized framework for identifying and targeting high-intent commercial and transactional keywords that drive trial sign-ups and coaching consultation bookings.

---

## 📋 2. Step-by-Step Keyword Research Process

### Step A: Identify Intent Categories
We group keywords into three categories based on user intent:
1.  **Transactional Intent (High Priority):** Users ready to buy or hire (e.g., "hire online personal trainer", "best online fitness coach cost").
2.  **Commercial Intent (Medium Priority):** Users evaluating options (e.g., "online fitness coaching reviews", "1-on-1 personal trainer app comparison").
3.  **Informational Intent (Low Priority - Excluded):** Users looking for free tips or information (e.g., "how to lose belly fat fast", "free exercises at home"). **We do not bid on informational keywords.**

### Step B: Run Google Keyword Planner
1.  Log in to Google Ads. Go to **Tools and Settings > Planning > Keyword Planner**.
2.  Select **Discover new keywords**. Enter seed phrases like `online personal trainer`, `custom workout program`, and `diet coaching program`.
3.  Filter the location targeting to your core markets (e.g., United States, United Kingdom, Canada).
4.  Export the keywords, search volume, competition, and top-of-page bids (low and high range).

### Step C: Match Type Strategy for Launch
To control budget spend at launch, follow these match type rules:
*   **Exact Match (`[keyword]`):** Use for highly specific, high-intent transactional terms (e.g., `[online fitness coach for busy professionals]`). This ensures our ads appear *only* for that exact query.
*   **Phrase Match (`"keyword"`):** Use for core service terms (e.g., `"online personal training"`). This allows search variations containing our target phrase (e.g., *best online personal training for weight loss*).
*   **Broad Match:** **Do not use at launch.** Broad match triggers ads for loosely related, low-intent terms, which wastes budget early on.

## 🚫 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Targeting Single-Word Keywords:** Bidding on broad terms like "fitness", "diet", or "workout". These are extremely expensive, highly competitive, and have low conversion intent. Stick to long-tail keywords (3+ words).
*   **Ignoring Competitor Brand Names:** Forgetting to run a search for competitor brand keywords. Monitoring competitor terms helps you identify what keywords they bid on and find positioning gaps.
*   **Skipping Location Targeting Checks:** Bidding on keywords globally without filtering search traffic to countries where your coaches are licensed or speak the language.
