# 20-Point Landing Page Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Checklist

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
Getting a user to click your ad is only the first step. If your landing page is slow, hard to read on mobile, or lacks trust signals, visitors will leave, which wastes your ad budget.

This **20-Point CRO Checklist** is a standard quality check for our web design and development teams. It ensures that every fitness landing page we publish is optimized for maximum conversions to help us reach our target CPA of **$50.00**.

---

## 📋 2. The 20-Point CRO Checklist

### A. Above the Fold (First Screen View)
*   [ ] **1. Single, Clear H1:** The main headline is clearly visible and matches the core search query.
*   [ ] **2. Supporting Subheadline:** Further explains the offer and builds interest immediately below the H1.
*   [ ] **3. Visual Hero Element:** A high-quality photo of a trainer, client check-in interface, or success story (no generic stock photos).
*   [ ] **4. Above-the-Fold Form/CTA:** The lead booking form or a prominent CTA button is visible without scrolling down.
*   [ ] **5. Trust Cues Above the Fold:** Include star ratings (e.g., "5.0 Rating on Google") or client badges near the main headline.

### B. Copywriting & Benefits
*   [ ] **6. Focus on Benefits, Not Features:** Focus copy on outcomes (e.g. "lose weight" or "gain energy") rather than catalog listings (e.g. "30 workouts in app").
*   [ ] **7. 3 Main Benefit Bullet Points:** Use bullet points with checkmark icons to make benefits easy to read.
*   [ ] **8. Plain Language:** Break down industry jargon (e.g., "hypertrophy cycles") into plain language ("build muscle").
*   [ ] **9. Clear Pricing/Value:** Position your premium value to justify the $150-$300/month cost.
*   [ ] **10. Clear Call-to-Action (CTA):** Use action-oriented CTA buttons (e.g., "Get My Fitness Plan") instead of generic terms ("Submit").

### C. Trust & Credibility
*   [ ] **11. Client Testimonial Reviews:** Include at least three reviews displaying client photos, names, and transformation reviews.
*   [ ] **12. Before & After Photos:** Include transformation photos showing real client results.
*   [ ] **13. Trainer Credentials:** Display certification badges (NASM, ACSM, ISSA) to establish expert authority.
*   [ ] **14. Privacy Assurance:** Add a short statement under the submit button (e.g. "We protect your data. No spam.") to build trust.
*   [ ] **15. FAQs Section:** Include 4-5 frequently asked questions to address common user objections (e.g. "Is nutrition included?").

### D. Forms & Technical SEO
*   [ ] **16. Short Lead Forms:** Keep forms to 3-4 fields max (Name, Email, Phone, Goal).
*   [ ] **17. Mobile-Friendly Layout:** Form inputs and buttons are large enough to tap easily on mobile screens.
*   [ ] **18. Remove Navigation Links:** Disable the main site navigation menu and header links to keep visitors focused on the page.
*   [ ] **19. Fast Load Times:** Compress images and optimize scripts to achieve mobile load times under **2.5 seconds** (LCP).
*   [ ] **20. Thank-You Page Redirect:** Ensure form submissions redirect to a confirmation page (`/thank-you`) containing Google conversion tracking tags.

## 🛠️ 3. Step-by-Step CRO Review SOP

1.  **Open Pre-Launch Preview:** Open the staging link of your landing page.
2.  **Audit on Mobile:** Test the landing page using your mobile phone or browser developer tools to verify mobile usability.
3.  **Cross-Check with the Checklist:** Go through all 20 items in this checklist. Mark items as checked off only when they meet the criteria.
4.  **Resolve Issues:** Fix any flagged issues before pushing the page live.

## 🚫 5. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Forgetting to Remove Header Menus:** Leaving your main website navigation menu at the top of landing pages, which lets visitors leave the page without converting.
*   **Using Complex Captcha Forms:** Adding complex math or text Captchas that make submitting forms frustrating for users. Use background spam filters (reCAPTCHA v3) instead.
*   **Bypassing Mobile Form Testing:** Deploying landing pages without verifying that the keyboard inputs work correctly on mobile screens.
