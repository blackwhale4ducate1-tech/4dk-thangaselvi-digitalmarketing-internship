# Landing Page Design & Copywriting Briefs

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
Paid search traffic is expensive. Sending visitors to a generic homepage leads to high bounce rates and low conversion rates. High-performing campaigns direct users to targeted **Landing Pages** that match their search intent.

This document contains design and copy briefs for **three landing pages** corresponding to our primary search campaigns: Brand Protection, Competitor Conquest, and Service & Intent. These briefs ensure our design and development teams build landing pages that align with our ad copy and drive conversions.

## 🛠️ 3. Step-by-Step Instructions: Launching Landing Pages

1.  **Draft Page Mockups:** Pass these copy briefs to your design team to create wireframes.
2.  **Ensure Above-The-Fold Form Placement:** Position your lead booking form or CTA button above the fold (visible without scrolling) next to the hero headline.
3.  **Implement Tracking Scripts:** Install your Google Tag Manager container to track form submissions as conversions.
4.  **A/B Testing Setup:** Set up A/B tests to test variations of your headlines and CTAs to optimize conversion rates over time.

## 🚫 5. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Including Site Navigation Header Links:** Leaving your main website navigation menu (Home, About, Blog, Contact) at the top of landing pages. This provides exit links that let visitors leave the page without converting. Remove all header and footer links.
*   **Long Multi-Step Forms:** Asking for too much information (e.g. asking for home address or fitness history) on the initial booking form. Keep forms short (3-4 fields max) to reduce friction.
*   **Slow Mobile Speeds:** Creating landing pages with heavy, uncompressed images that load slowly on mobile networks. Slow load speeds cause high bounce rates.
